Independent AI & NLP Research

Building trustworthy language technologies for real‑world problems.

Ramanux Labs is an independent research initiative based in Guwahati, Assam, India, focused on Large Language Models, Natural Language Processing, Legal AI, low-resource languages, and AI systems for critical domains. The lab publishes technical research, develops open-source prototypes, and contributes to applied AI for real-world deployment.

Current Projects

BharatLex

Status: Benchmark & interactive explorer

A bilingual Hindi–English retrieval benchmark for the Bharatiya Sanhitas 2023 (BNS, BNSS, BSA): 1,059 aligned sections, 2,364 subsection queries and 3,610 questions with verified answer spans. Open the explorer ↓

DuoMind

Status: Research Prototype

An offline AI assistant for policy, compliance and domain-specific reasoning with traceable responses and privacy-first deployment.

BharatLex Explorer

An interactive 3D map of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita and Sakshya Adhiniyam. Click a section to read the Hindi and English text side by side, search by section or topic, and find a path between two provisions. Links are derived from cross-references, keyword themes and text similarity.

About Ramanux Labs

Ramanux Labs is an independent AI and NLP research initiative based in Guwahati, Assam, India. The lab develops research prototypes, publishes technical knowledge, and contributes open-source software for applied artificial intelligence — with a focus on large language models, retrieval-augmented generation, and legal AI systems.
